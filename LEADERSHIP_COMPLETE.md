## API Endpoints

- `GET /api/leadership` - All leaders
- `GET /api/leadership/:id` - Specific leader
- `GET /api/leadership/advisor` - Advisor details
- `GET /api/leadership/accountant` - Accountant details
- `GET /api/leadership/owner` - School owner details
