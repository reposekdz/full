### Chapter 5: Creating Your Student Account

#### 5.1 Account Registration Process

**Step 1: Receiving Your Credentials**

When you're admitted to Garden TVET School, you'll receive:
- Student ID number
- Temporary password
- Registration link
- Welcome email
- Quick start guide

**Step 2: First-Time Login**

1. Visit the school website: www.gardentvet.rw
2. Click on "Student Portal" in the header
3. Enter your Student ID
4. Enter your temporary password
5. Click "Login"

**Step 3: Password Setup**

For security, you must change your password on first login:
- Minimum 8 characters
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include at least one number
- Include at least one special character
- Cannot be a previously used password

**Example of Strong Password:**
- ❌ Weak: password123
- ❌ Weak: StudentName
- ✅ Strong: Gard3n@TVET2026!

**Step 4: Profile Completion**

Complete your profile with:
- Personal information
- Contact details
- Emergency contacts
- Profile photo
- Academic preferences
- Communication preferences

**Step 5: Security Setup**

Enable additional security:
- Two-factor authentication (2FA)
- Security questions
- Recovery email
- Recovery phone number
- Trusted devices

#### 5.2 Understanding Your Student ID

Your Student ID is your unique identifier in the system:

**Format:** YEAR + SEQUENTIAL NUMBER

**What Your Student ID Gives You Access To:**

**Protecting Your Student ID:**

#### 6.3 Understanding Dashboard Metrics

**Academic Performance Indicators**

**GPA (Grade Point Average)**
- Scale: 0.0 - 4.0
- Calculation: Weighted average of all courses
- Updates: After each grade entry
- Importance: Academic standing indicator

**Attendance Percentage**
- Calculation: (Present days / Total days) × 100
- Minimum required: 75%
- Warning threshold: Below 80%
- Critical threshold: Below 75%

**Assignment Completion Rate**
- Calculation: (Submitted / Total) × 100
- Target: 100%
- Impact: Affects final grades
- Tracking: Real-time updates

**Exam Performance**
- Average exam scores
- Subject-wise breakdown
- Trend analysis
- Comparison with class average
- Improvement suggestions
