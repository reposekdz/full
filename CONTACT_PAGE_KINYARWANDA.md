## FAQ Translations:

1. "What are the admission requirements?" → "Ni ibihe bisabwa kugirango winjire?"
   Answer: "Kugirango usabe kwinjira, ukeneye impamyabushobozi ya O-Level, indangamuntu, amafoto y'irangamuntu, n'icyangombwa cy'amavuko. Sura ibiro by'injira kugirango ubone ibisabwa byuzuye."

2. "How can I pay school fees?" → "Nishyura nte amafaranga y'ishuri?"
   Answer: "Amafaranga y'ishuri ashobora kwishyurwa binyuze kuri Mobile Money (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money), kuri banki, cyangwa ku biro by'amafaranga. Ushobora kandi gushyiraho gahunda yo kwishyura buhoro buhoro."

3. "What programs do you offer?" → "Ni izihe porogaramu mutanga?"
   Answer: "Dutanga porogaramu z'ikoranabuhanga mu iterambere rya software, ubwubatsi, n'ikoranabuhanga ry'ibinyabiziga. Buri porogaramu imara imyaka 3 ifite amahugurwa y'ibanze n'ay'umwuga."

4. "What are the office hours?" → "Ni ayahe masaha ibiro bifungura?"
   Answer: "Ibiro byacu bifungura kuwa mbere kugeza kuwa gatanu, saa 2 z'igitondo kugeza saa 11 z'umugoroba, na kuwa gatandatu saa 3 z'igitondo kugeza saa 7 z'umugoroba. Tufunze ku cyumweru n'iminsi mikuru."

5. "How do I access my student portal?" → "Njya gute kuri portal y'umunyeshuri?"
   Answer: "Sura urubuga rwacu kandi ukande kuri 'Portal y'Umunyeshuri' mu mutwe. Koresha ID yawe y'umunyeshuri n'ijambo ry'ibanga wahawe mu gihe cyo kwiyandikisha. Hamagara ubufasha bwa IT niba ufite ikibazo cyo kwinjira."

6. "Is there accommodation available?" → "Hari aho guturamo?"
   Answer: "Yego, dufite amacumbi yo guturamo ku ishuri ku bagabo n'abagore. Amafaranga yo guturamo atandukanye n'ay'ishuri. Hamagara serivisi z'abanyeshuri kugirango umenye niba hari umwanya."
